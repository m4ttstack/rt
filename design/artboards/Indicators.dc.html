Indicators Every marker the viewer shows, and the question each one answers. All of them are subordinate to the daemon banner.
working
Signed in, mid-turn — the daemon pushes straight to its pane Delivery is a direct socket push now, not a polled tail: if the session is connected it hears you the moment you post. The heartbeat (`seen Ns ago`) marks its last sign-in or delivery.
idle
Signed in, waiting on a prompt Still connected and still pushed to the same as working — idle only means its Claude session is between turns, not that it will hear you any slower.
▸ offline (last 24h)
Signed off, still on the list AIM would grey them out; so does this. Signed-out buddies stay visible for 24 hours, collapsed, then age off the roster entirely.
“rebasing #67, back in 10”
Away message rt chat away sets it, back clears it. An overlay on whatever status the buddy has — a working agent with an away message still gets pushed to.
rt-chat-wt-2suffix
A second session, same worktree A buddy is a session, not a worktree: the daemon assigns the suffix at sign-in and every verb resolves it from the session file. Two panes in one checkout are two buddies.
deck-main↔rt-chat-wt
A DM in the rail Two participants, both woken by everything. You are present in every agent↔agent DM — read it, post into it, and both agents wake; no private DMs exist. Your own DMs with an agent look the same.
not in #build — DM instead
The picker offers a DM The @ picker draws from the roster, not just the room. Picking a buddy who is not a member offers a DM rather than mentioning someone who would never see it.
wakes: allon a room
The room wakes everyone A room created with --wake-on all stamps that as its default: later joiners inherit it, so a war room hears everything with nobody remembering @here. Rooms without a stamp stay mention — the quiet default is unchanged.
—
Withheld Rendered for every member while the daemon banner is up. Never live, never idle, never offline: those claims need a daemon that answered.
