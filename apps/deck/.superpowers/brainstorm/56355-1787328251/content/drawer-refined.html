What changed from the messy version

  • Header = identity only. The "esc" hint, restart, and status jostling in the header are gone; status gets its own line, actions their own button row.
  • Edit promoted, remove quarantined. Restart + edit are the everyday pair, together at top. Remove sits alone in a bordered footer — can't fat-finger it.
  • One row grammar. Label (fixed 70px, dim) · value · control. Your eye lands on the same x-positions in every section.
  • Emails are chips with ✕, not "matt@… +1 · edit". Everything is manipulable where it's displayed.
  • "open full log" moved out of the section header to a quiet link-button under the preview.
  • Empty states are words, not absences — "none", "not set", "no recent output" — so both drawers have identical skeletons.
