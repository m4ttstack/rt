Rules the atlas encodes

  • Same skeleton per row kind — apps always show public / nav / actions / danger; services and the tunnel show reduced roots. No layout shift between apps.
  • Editing always gets its own screen with save in the nav bar and inputs in grouped rows — never inline editing on a reading screen.
  • Destructive = red row, then action sheet stating the blast radius (remove app); remove password is a red row inside the password screen, no sheet (recoverable).
  • Errors ride the surface they belong to: sync failure is a banner on the root; crash output turns the logs hint red.
  • Transient states replace their trigger — restart row becomes a spinner row; nothing else moves.
