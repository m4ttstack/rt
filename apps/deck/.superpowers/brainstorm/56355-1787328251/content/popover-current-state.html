What I see wrong in the new popover

A

Stray "revert to 11007" tooltip on open

The popover moves focus to the revert button, and kit tooltips show on focus — so a tooltip hangs below the popover the moment it opens. Worst artifact in the shot.

B

Clipped fragment at the popover's top-right

A sliver of something (looks like the DEV chip's own tooltip, clipped by the table's overflow container) pokes out beside the health badge.

C

The popover itself is plain / cramped

Grey box, "normally 11007" label, revert + switch jammed on one row. Functional, but not obviously better-looking than what it replaced. Worth a design pass on its content.

D

Placement / anchoring

It opens below-left of the chip and floats over the section heading. Possibly fine (popovers overlay), but check it feels anchored to the chip.
