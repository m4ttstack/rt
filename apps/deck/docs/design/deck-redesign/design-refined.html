Round 2 — the fork is smaller than it looked

Refining A and C revealed they share the same table: status-first columns, leading health dot, calm right side. What actually differs is where per-app controls live — a drawer, or in-place expansion. Both full boards below, then the choice.

Shared table refinements (both directions get these)
  • Leading health dot on the site name — job ① reads down the left edge, no mid-row badge hunting
  • Columns regrouped: identity (site) · facts (port, health, service) · controls (public + …) — status left, action right, one divider of whitespace
  • Access glyph pair → text state: "open" (dim) / "password" / "2 emails" — reads without hovering
  • Error text stays inline under the site name (it's status, not control) but in one consistent slot
  • Stat strip keeps its one-line text form, healthy count bolded green when all-green, red fraction otherwise

A′ — Drawer, refined

Row = status + public switch + restart (kept inline — it's your #3 most-used action and one click matters when something's down). Everything else in the drawer: click row or press enter. Access management moves into the drawer (password + email list inline), retiring the AccessModal. Stderr tail lives there too, with "open full" for the modal. Esc or ✕ closes; the open row stays highlighted.
